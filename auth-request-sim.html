    What to do in real life

    • Say yes only to a sign-in you started yourself, seconds ago.
    • Your own city and your own kind of device prove nothing. If you did not start it, it is not yours.
    • If requests keep arriving, say no every time and change your password straight away. Somebody already has the old one.
    • Nobody who telephones you needs your code. Not your bank, not the police, not support.
