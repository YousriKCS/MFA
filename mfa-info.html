Authentication Request Simulator

Imagine that an authentication request suddenly appears on your phone. It asks you to approve a login to one of your accounts.

Some requests are connected to logins that you started. Others may come from someone who has obtained your password and is trying to access your account.

What you will do

  1. Read the authentication request. Look carefully at the account, location, device and time shown in the request.
  2. Decide whether you started the login. Think about whether you are currently trying to access that account.

    3. Sometimes you may receive an authentication request when you are not trying to log in.

    In this simulation, you will practise deciding whether you should approve or reject different login requests.

    Remember: If you are not trying to log in, reject the request. Someone else may be trying to access your account.
