Authentication Request Simulator

A request appears on your phone asking you to approve a login. Some of these come from logins you started yourself. Others come from someone who already has your password and is trying to get into your account.

Telling the two apart is the whole skill, and it takes about ten seconds.

What you will do

  1. Read the request. Look at the account, the place it is coming from, the device, and the time.
  2. Ask whether you started it. If you are not signing in to that account right now, you did not start it.
  3. Approve or refuse. Only ever approve a request you started yourself.
  4. Read what happened. Each decision is explained, along with what it would have meant for the account.

This is a safe practice page. No real accounts are used, and nothing you decide here is stored or sent anywhere.
